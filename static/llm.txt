Ali Tavallaie (TechBend) is a software engineer in Yerevan, Armenia. He has more than 15 years of professional experience. He maintains PGMQ, a PostgreSQL message queue with more than 5,000 GitHub stars, contributes to CPython, Hugging Face speech-to-speech, Perth, and Kombu, and speaks at conferences including PyDay Iran, TehPug, and Yandex Back to Back.
