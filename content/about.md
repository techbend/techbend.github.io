I'm Ali Tavallaie, a software engineer. I build AI systems and open-source software, working across generative AI, audio technology, voice agents, MLOps, and AI infrastructure. I'm particularly interested in building the distributed systems that make AI workloads reliable, scalable, and capable of running over long periods of time.

I have been building software for more than 15 years. I am a [PyTorch Foundation Ambassador](https://pytorch.org/blog/meet-the-2026-pytorch-foundation-ambassadors/) for the 2026–2028 cohort, and a member of the [Python Software Foundation Diversity and Inclusion Workgroup](https://diversity.python.org). I maintain [PGMQ](https://github.com/pgmq/pgmq), a message queue on PostgreSQL with more than 5,000 GitHub stars.

## Teaching

I help and mentor students in different groups. I am part of the [PyTeach](https://pyteach.me/) team, which teaches Python through lessons, coding challenges, and a path that feels like a game.
